Privacy Policy for Moodbloom

Developer: Bajrangi · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Moodbloom (package name com.bajrangi.moodtracker), a mood tracking app for Android published on Google Play by Bajrangi ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Moodbloom does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

The moods, notes and other entries you record, along with your settings and reminder preferences, are stored only on your device. The app does not have internet access, so this data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Third-party services

Moodbloom does not use advertising, analytics or any other third-party services that collect data.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Your choices

You can delete all of your data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Moodbloom does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Security

Because no data leaves your device, nothing is stored on our servers. Your entries are protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
