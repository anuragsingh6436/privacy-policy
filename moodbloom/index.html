Privacy Policy for Moodbloom

Developer: Bajrangi · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Moodbloom (package name com.bajrangi.moodtracker), a mood tracking app for Android published on Google Play by Bajrangi ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Moodbloom does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

The moods, notes and other entries you record, along with your settings and reminder preferences, are stored only on your device. Your moods and notes never leave your device: they are not sent to us or to any third party, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The app uses the internet only for the crash reports and usage statistics described below, which you can turn off.

Third-party services

Moodbloom does not show advertising. It uses the following Google services, which may collect and process certain information automatically:

  • Firebase Crashlytics – to receive crash reports so we can find and fix bugs.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – to see, in aggregate, how the app is used.

We do not sell your information, and we do not receive personal information from these services.

Crash reports and usage statistics

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: the crash log (stack trace), your device model, Android version, app version and a Firebase installation ID. Google Analytics for Firebase records app interactions such as an entry being saved (only that one was saved, never which mood you picked or what you wrote) and taps on the share buttons, together with an app-instance ID and basic device information; we only look at these statistics in aggregate. Neither service receives your moods, notes, name or other personal information from us.

This data is processed by Google on our behalf and is encrypted in transit. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

Turning it off: on the Home screen, tap the settings icon (top right) and switch off Send diagnostics. Crash reports and usage statistics then stop being collected from your device. Deleting it: to ask us to delete the crash reports and usage statistics linked to your device, email bajrangi.games6436@gmail.com.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Your choices

You can turn off crash reports and usage statistics at any time in the app under Settings → Send diagnostics (the settings icon on the Home screen). You can delete all of your data on the device by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Moodbloom does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Security

Your moods and notes never leave your device and are protected by your device's own security. We do not run servers of our own; crash reports and usage statistics are stored by Google (Firebase) and protected according to Google's security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact Bajrangi at bajrangi.games6436@gmail.com.
