Child Safety Standards – Slam Book: Dosti Diary

Developer: Bajrangi · Package: com.bajrangi.slambook · Last updated: October 11, 2026

Our commitment

Bajrangi has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any content or behaviour that sexualises minors. Such content and behaviour are strictly prohibited in Slam Book: Dosti Diary and on its website (bajrangi-slambook.web.app).

How the app limits risk

  • The app is not designed for children under 13.
  • There is no public feed, chat, messaging, search for people or friend discovery. A slam book page can only be written by someone who received the book owner's private link, and only the book owner can read it.
  • Users cannot upload photos, videos or files. Pages contain only a nickname and short text answers.
  • Questions never ask for phone numbers, addresses, school names, age, birthdays or photos.
  • Book owners can delete any page and can turn off "Accept new pages" at any time, which stops their link from accepting new pages.

Reporting

Anyone can report child safety concerns, or any abusive or inappropriate content, by emailing bajrangi.games6436@gmail.com with the subject "Slam Book child safety report". Please include the slam book link (if any) and a description of the concern. Reports are reviewed promptly, with child safety reports given the highest priority.

Our response

  • We remove reported content that violates these standards and disable the slam book link involved.
  • We block access for accounts involved in CSAE.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and, in India, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), as required by applicable law, and we cooperate with law enforcement.

Compliance

Slam Book: Dosti Diary complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
