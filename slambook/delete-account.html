Delete your Slam Book account and data

App: Slam Book: Dosti Diary (com.bajrangi.slambook) · Developer: Bajrangi · Last updated: October 11, 2026

Slam Book: Dosti Diary works without a login. Behind the scenes the app uses an anonymous account, and if you turned on "Back up with Google" that account is linked to the Google account you chose.

How to request deletion

  1. Email bajrangi.games6436@gmail.com with the subject "Slam Book – delete my account".
  2. Include the email address of the Google account used for "Back up with Google" (if you used it), or your slam book link (https://bajrangi-slambook.web.app/b/…). You can copy the link with "Send invite" in the app.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (anonymous user ID and, if linked, the Google sign-in link).
  • Your Google backup: your slam book (name, questions, settings) and all backed-up pages.
  • Your online slam book and any pages still waiting to be collected; your slam book link stops working.
  • Your notification token.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Standard Firebase/Google Cloud backups and logs may hold traces for up to 30 days before they expire. Pages saved on your phone stay there until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Delete a page: open the page and tap the delete (bin) icon. If backup is on, it is removed from the backup too.
  • Stop new pages: on the home screen turn off "Accept new pages" — your link no longer accepts pages.
  • A page you wrote in someone else's book: email us with that slam book link and your nickname and we will remove it if it is still on our server.
