Privacy Policy for Slam Book: Dosti Diary

Developer: Bajrangi · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Slam Book: Dosti Diary (package name com.bajrangi.slambook), a digital slam book for friends for Android published on Google Play by Bajrangi ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Slam Book: Dosti Diary does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The only identifying information is the name or nickname you choose to write on your slam book or on a friend's page.

Your slam book, the questions you pick and the pages your friends write are stored on your device.

Slam book links. When you tap "Send invite", the app creates a link to your slam book. To make the link work, your book's name (the name or nickname on the cover), the list of questions you picked and your language setting are stored on our server (Google Firebase Cloud Firestore). Anyone who has the link can see that name and those questions and add a page; nobody can see the pages except you.

Pages sent by link. When a friend fills your slam book through the link (in the browser at bajrangi-slambook.web.app or in the app), their name or nickname, the page colour and sticker they chose and their answers are sent to our server and held there only until your app collects them. As soon as your app copies a page to your phone, it is deleted from the server. Only your app can read these pages.

Notifications. If you share a slam book link and allow notifications, the app stores a notification token for your phone (Firebase Cloud Messaging) together with your language setting, so we can tell you when a friend fills your book. The token identifies the app installation, not you. Turning off notifications in Android settings stops them.

Google backup (optional). If you tap "Back up with Google" and choose a Google account, your slam book (its name, questions and settings) and all its pages are stored on our server under that account, so you can restore them on a new phone. We receive the email address of the Google account you choose, only to show which account the backup belongs to. Only you can read your backup. To delete it, contact us at the email below.

Codes. If you share without internet, an invite or answer code is created on the device and sent only through the app you choose (for example a messaging app), so only the people you share it with can see it.

The app and the web page sign in anonymously (Firebase Anonymous Authentication) so that only you can read your pages. This creates a random user ID that is not linked to your name, email or phone number. Pages on your phone are deleted when you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Please don't write sensitive personal details such as phone numbers or addresses in answers.

Third-party services

Slam Book: Dosti Diary uses the following third-party services, which may collect and process certain information automatically:

  • Google AdMob – to show banner advertisements. AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data in order to serve, measure and personalize ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in advertising.
  • Google Firebase (Remote Config) – to remotely enable or disable app features. Firebase may collect a Firebase installation ID and basic device information. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.
  • Firebase Crashlytics – to find and fix crashes. If the app crashes, a crash report is sent with the crash log, device model, Android and app version and a Firebase installation ID. See Crash reports and usage statistics.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used (slam book pages saved or sent and taps on the share and invite buttons (never the names or answers written in them)). It uses an app-instance ID and basic device information. See Crash reports and usage statistics.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Hosting, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) – to make slam book links, notifications and the optional Google backup work: an anonymous user ID (or your Google account if you turn on backup), the slam book details described above, pages waiting to be collected or backed up, and a notification token. Data is stored in Google Cloud (region asia-south1, Mumbai) and encrypted in transit and at rest. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

We do not sell your information, and we do not receive personal information from these services.

Crash reports and usage statistics

To keep Slam Book stable and improve it, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, both provided and processed by Google:

  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): when the app crashes, it sends the crash log (stack trace), device model, Android version, app version and a Firebase installation ID.
  • Usage statistics (Analytics): app interactions such as slam book pages saved or sent and taps on the share and invite buttons (never the names or answers written in them), together with an app-instance ID and basic device information. We look only at aggregated statistics.

We never send your name, anything you type, or other personal information with these reports. The data is processed by Google and encrypted in transit. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

Turning it off: switch off Send diagnostics on the home screen in the app. Crash reports and usage statistics then stop being sent from your device.

Deleting it: to ask for crash and usage data collected from your device to be deleted, email bajrangi.games6436@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can delete all locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Slam Book: Dosti Diary does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Security

Slam book data on our server is protected by access rules: a book can only be changed by the phone that created it, and pages can only be read or deleted by the book's owner. Pages sent by link are removed from the server as soon as the owner's app collects them; pages in an optional Google backup stay until the backup is deleted. To have your online slam book or a page you wrote removed, contact us at the email below. Data handled by third-party services is protected according to their own security practices.
