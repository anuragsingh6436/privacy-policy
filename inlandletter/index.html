Privacy Policy for Inland Letter: Chitthi Likho

Developer: Bajrangi · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Inland Letter: Chitthi Likho (package name com.bajrangi.inlandletter), an app to write letters and postcards to friends in the style of an Indian inland letter for Android published on Google Play by Bajrangi ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Inland Letter does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

The letters and postcards you write or receive (your mailbox) and your settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you choose to share a picture card, the image is created on your device and shared only with the app you pick. The content of letters you send is placed in the link itself, as explained below.

Shared letter links

When you send a letter or postcard, the app creates a link to https://anuragsingh6436.github.io/letter/. The letter's content is packed into the part of the link after the # sign. Web browsers do not send that part to the web server, so the letter is decoded only in the browser or app of the person who opens the link. We do not run a database and do not store your letters on any server.

Anyone who has the link can read the letter, so share it only with the person you mean to write to and do not include sensitive personal information. The web page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; like any website, GitHub and Google may receive your IP address and basic browser information when the page is opened (see the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google's Privacy Policy).

Third-party services

Inland Letter uses the following third-party services, which may collect and process certain information automatically:

  • Google AdMob – to show advertisements. AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data in order to serve, measure and personalize ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in advertising.
  • Google Firebase (Remote Config) – to remotely enable or disable app features. Firebase may collect a Firebase installation ID and basic device information. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

We do not sell your information, and we do not receive personal information from these services.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can delete all locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Inland Letter does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Security

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, none is stored on our servers. Data handled by third-party services is protected according to their own security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
