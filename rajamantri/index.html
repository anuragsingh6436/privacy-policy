Privacy Policy for Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi

Developer: Bajrangi · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi (package name com.bajrangi.rajamantri), a Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi chit game for Android published on Google Play by Bajrangi ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Player names you type, scores and settings (theme, sound, rules, rounds) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you choose to share a result card, the image is created on your device and shared only with the app you pick.

Third-party services

Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi uses the following third-party services, which may collect and process certain information automatically:

  • Google AdMob – to show banner advertisements. AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data in order to serve, measure and personalize ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in advertising.
  • Google Firebase (Remote Config) – to remotely enable or disable app features. Firebase may collect a Firebase installation ID and basic device information. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.
  • Firebase Crashlytics – to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. A crash report contains the crash log (stack trace), device model, Android version, app version and a Firebase installation ID.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used (for example games started and finished, and taps on the share buttons). It uses an app-instance ID and basic device information. Player names and anything you type are never sent.

We do not sell your information, and we do not receive personal information from these services.

Crash reports and usage statistics

Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi uses Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase (described above) to collect crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. This information is processed by Google on our behalf, is encrypted in transit, and is used only to keep the app stable and improve it. It is not used for advertising and is not sold.

You can turn this off at any time: open the app's home screen and switch Send diagnostics off. To ask us to delete diagnostics data linked to your device, email bajrangi.games6436@gmail.com.

Your choices

You can stop crash reports and usage statistics by switching Send diagnostics off on the app's home screen. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can delete all locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Deleting your data

Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or other contact details. To delete your data:

  • Data on your phone: your progress and settings are stored only on your device. Clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the app to delete all of it.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: first turn off Send diagnostics in the app so nothing new is sent. Then email bajrangi.games6436@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data – Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi”. We will delete the crash and usage data we can link to your installation within 30 days and reply to confirm. Independently of any request, Crashlytics deletes crash reports automatically after 90 days and Google Analytics deletes event-level data automatically under its data-retention setting.
  • Advertising data: data used by Google AdMob is tied to your device's advertising ID. To delete it, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

What is deleted: the crash reports and usage events linked to your installation. What is kept: only aggregated totals (for example, how many times a screen was opened in a day) that cannot be linked to you.

Children's privacy

Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Security

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, none is stored on our servers. Data handled by third-party services is protected according to their own security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
