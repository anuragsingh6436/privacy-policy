Privacy Policy for Name Place Animal Thing

Developer: Bajrangi · Last updated: October 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Name Place Animal Thing (package name com.bajrangi.nameplace), a Name Place Animal Thing word game for Android published on Google Play by Bajrangi ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Name Place Animal Thing does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Player names and the answers you type, scores, statistics and settings (theme, sound, timer, rounds) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The only exception is the optional online word check described below, which sends a single answer word (never your name or anything about you) to a public encyclopedia or dictionary. If you choose to share a result card, the image is created on your device and shared only with the app you pick.

Online word check

The app first checks answers against its built-in word list, which works offline. When an answer is not in the built-in list ("Teacher says ❓"), and your device is online, the app looks up that one word to see whether it is a real name, place, animal or thing:

  • Names, places and animals are looked up on Wikidata, run by the Wikimedia Foundation. See the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Things are looked up on the Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev).

Only the answer word itself is sent, together with the app's name and version. No player names, account, advertising ID or other identifier is sent by the app. As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. The result is remembered on your device so the same word is not looked up again. You can turn this off at any time in the app's settings ("Check unknown words online"); the game then works fully offline.

Third-party services

Name Place Animal Thing uses the following third-party services, which may collect and process certain information automatically:

  • Google AdMob – to show banner and full-screen (interstitial) advertisements. AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data in order to serve, measure and personalize ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in advertising.
  • Google Firebase (Remote Config) – to remotely enable or disable app features. Firebase may collect a Firebase installation ID and basic device information. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

We do not sell your information, and we do not receive personal information from these services.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can delete all locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Name Place Animal Thing does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Security

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, none is stored on our servers. Data handled by third-party services is protected according to their own security practices.
