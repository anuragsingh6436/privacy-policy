Privacy Policy – Gilli Danda 3D

App: Gilli Danda 3D (com.bajrangi.gillidanda3d) · Developer: Bajrangi Games
Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short:
  • No account, no login, no sign-up.
  • We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or photos, and we do not sell personal data.
  • Your scores and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • The game plays offline. It shows ads from Google AdMob (between innings, never during a shot), which may use your device's advertising ID, and it sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics to Google Firebase. You can turn the crash reports and usage statistics off in Settings → "Send diagnostics", and change your ad consent in Settings → "Ads & privacy".
  • Sharing a score card happens only when you tap Share, through your phone's own share screen.

1. Who we are

Gilli Danda 3D is a game made by Bajrangi Games, an independent game studio in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game on Android. If you have any question, write to us at bajrangi.games6436@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. There is no account system, no online multiplayer and no chat. "With friends" mode is pass-and-play on one phone, and the player names you type stay on that phone and are never sent anywhere.

What the game does send, through Google's services described in section 6, is limited to:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): if the game crashes or hits an internal error, a report with the technical details of the error, your device model, Android version, app version, free memory, and a few game facts such as the mode you were in (for example "daily") and whether realistic kids were on. Crash reports contain a Crashlytics installation ID; they do not contain your name or any typed text.
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): a few anonymous events about how the game is used, such as that the app was opened, which mode was started, how an innings ended (the score and the way you were out), whether the scoop was safe, which share button was tapped, and whether an ad was shown or rewarded. These are sent with a Firebase app-instance ID and basic device and app information. No names, scores tied to you personally, or free text.
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob): your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information, used to show and measure ads (see section 6).
  • App configuration and updates (Firebase Remote Config and Google Play In-App Updates): a request for the game's current settings (for example whether ads are on) and for available updates, with a Firebase installation ID and the app version.

You can switch the crash reports and usage statistics off at any time in the game's Settings → Send diagnostics (on by default). Ads and the configuration request cannot be switched off from inside the game, but you can control ad personalisation as described in section 6.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a small save file inside its own private app storage on your phone. It contains:

  • your scores, best shots, Daily Challenge streak and Village Tournament progress;
  • the kit you have unlocked (danda woods, gilli paints, outfits);
  • your settings (language, sound, vibration, Easy mode and similar options) and any player names you entered for pass-and-play.

This file never leaves your phone through the game. We cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Gilli Danda 3D → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the game. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include app data in your backup under your Google account settings; that is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

The Daily Challenge is created from your phone's local date. It does not need the internet and does not send anything anywhere.

4. Sharing your score (only when you choose)

On a result screen you can tap Story, WhatsApp or More. When you do, the game draws a picture of your result on your phone (for example "87 DANDAS!") and hands that image, a short line of text and a link to the game's Google Play page to the app you choose through Android's standard share feature. Story opens Instagram's "Add to story" screen if Instagram is installed.

  • Nothing is shared unless you tap a share button, and you can still cancel in the app you picked.
  • The image contains only game content: your score, the in-game village and the fictional characters. It does not contain your name, photo or any personal details.
  • We do not receive a copy of what you share. Once you post or send it, it is handled by that app (such as Instagram or WhatsApp) under its own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

The game asks for no special permissions. It uses the standard vibrate permission for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or your photo gallery.

6. Advertising, analytics, crash reports and app configuration

To keep the game free, Gilli Danda 3D shows ads from Google AdMob: a short full-screen ad now and then when you leave a result screen (never during a shot, never in Practice, and not in your first session), and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch after an OUT in Play or the Tournament for one extra life ("Ek aur mauka?"). You can always say no to the rewarded video. The game also uses Firebase Remote Config (by Google) to switch features such as ads on or off and to tell you about updates without an app update, Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports, Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage statistics, and Google Play In-App Updates to offer an update from inside the game.

These Google services may collect and process, on their own servers:

  • your device's advertising ID and IP address, to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud (AdMob);
  • a Firebase installation ID / app-instance ID and basic device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, language, screen size), to deliver the right configuration and to count usage (Remote Config, Analytics);
  • crash and error reports with technical details of the crash, the device state and the game facts listed in section 2 (Crashlytics);
  • the usage events listed in section 2 (Analytics);
  • diagnostic and interaction data about ads (for example whether an ad was shown, closed or rewarded).

We do not receive your name, email or any other information that identifies you personally, and we do not sell personal data. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Firebase Analytics is configured not to use ad-personalisation signals. You can learn how Google uses this data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy; Google's retention periods apply (Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days, Analytics event data for up to 14 months).

Your choices:

  • Settings → Send diagnostics (in the game): turns crash reports and usage statistics off (or on again). It is on by default.
  • Settings → Ads & privacy (in the game): where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and this entry lets you review or change your choice at any time.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

7. Children's privacy

Gilli Danda 3D is a game about a traditional Indian childhood sport, made for teenagers and adults who remember playing it. It is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; ads are requested with Google's "not child-directed" setting and a maximum content rating of PG. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data security and retention

We run no servers of our own: the crash reports, usage statistics and ad requests described above go to Google's services, encrypted in transit, and are kept by Google for its standard periods (section 6). We see them only as aggregated dashboards and crash listings in the Firebase and AdMob consoles. The save file on your phone stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to reply, and delete it on request.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the GDPR or similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. The game sends us nothing that identifies you by name, so most requests need no action on our side; to stop future collection use Settings → Send diagnostics and the ad choices in section 6, and to have Google delete data tied to your advertising ID reset the ID in Android Settings. You are welcome to contact us with any request and we will answer.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes are made before the app version that needs them is released. Version 0.7 (October 2026) added ads, crash reports, usage statistics and in-app updates.
