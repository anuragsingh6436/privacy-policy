Privacy Policy for Bingo Rough Copy

Developer: Bajrangi · Last updated: October 9, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Bingo Rough Copy (package name com.bajrangi.bingo), a handwritten-grid Bingo game for Android published on Google Play by Bajrangi ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Bingo Rough Copy does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Player names you type, your grids, game statistics and settings (theme, sound, number of bots) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you choose to share a result card, the image is created on your device and shared only with the app you pick.

Third-party services

Bingo Rough Copy uses the following third-party services, which may collect and process certain information automatically:

  • Google AdMob – to show banner advertisements. AdMob may collect the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data in order to serve, measure and personalize ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in advertising.
  • Google Firebase (Remote Config) – to remotely enable or disable app features. Firebase may collect a Firebase installation ID and basic device information. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

We do not sell your information, and we do not receive personal information from these services.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can delete all locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Bingo Rough Copy does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

Security

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, none is stored on our servers. Data handled by third-party services is protected according to their own security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
